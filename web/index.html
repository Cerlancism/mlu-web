Motherload Unlimited

The fan-made mining games by alexxz4, in your browser.

  • MLU 1 Version 3.1, Shaken Snowglobe Edition (2008). Dig deep under Mars and restart the planet's core.
  • MLU 2: Crematoria Summer 2009 release. Mine eastward across the scorching planet Crematoria.

Both games in one app on your home screen or in your app list, working offline too.

To install both games as an app on an iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Fan remakes of the Flash game Motherload, ported from Java to the web. Saved games stay in your browser.
